I am a PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at University of Minnesota (UMN), advised by Prof. Changhyun Choi. In the Choice Robotics Lab at the Minnesota Robotics Institute (MnRI), I study Vision-Language-Action models, generative policies, and contact-rich manipulation.

From May to September 2026, I was a research intern on the Optimization & Robotics Team at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL). I developed learning-based methods for robotic disassembly under pose uncertainty, integrating learned manipulation policies with compliant control and evaluating them on an industrial robot.

Before joining UMN, I was a research engineer at Hanwha Aerospace (January–July 2024), where I built Isaac Sim environments and supported field testing for autonomous unmanned ground vehicles. I completed my master's and bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), advised by Hyungpil Moon.
